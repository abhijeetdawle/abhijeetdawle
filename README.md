🌐 About Me

I am Dr. Abhijeet Dawle, an Assistant Professor in the Basic Sciences & Humanities Department at SVKM's NMIMS – Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering (MPSTME), Shirpur.

My academic work brings together English Language & Literature, Communication, Human Resource Management, Management, Technology, Artificial Intelligence and Education.

My research focuses on understanding how technology and human-centric approaches can transform education, workplaces, HR practices and the future of work.

I believe in research that crosses disciplinary boundaries and creates meaningful connections between people, technology and knowledge.
