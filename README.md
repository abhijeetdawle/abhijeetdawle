👋 About Me


I am Dr. Abhijeet Dawle, an Assistant Professor in the Basic Sciences and Humanities Department at SVKM’s NMIMS, Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering (MPSTME), Shirpur.

My academic and research interests span English Language and Literature, Communication Skills, Human Resource Management, Management, African Women’s Writing, Industry 5.0, Sustainable HR Practices, and interdisciplinary research.

I am passionate about teaching, research, academic writing, mentoring students, and developing innovative approaches to engineering education.
