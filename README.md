🌟 About Me

I am Dr. Abhijeet Dawle, an Assistant Professor in the Basic Sciences & Humanities Department at SVKM’s NMIMS – Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering (MPSTME), Shirpur.

With a strong interdisciplinary academic background, my work connects English Language & Literature, Communication, Management, Human Resource Management, Technology, and Education.

I am particularly interested in exploring how emerging technologies and human-centric approaches can transform education, workplaces, and HR practices.
