🌐 About Me

<table> <tr> <td width="65%" valign="top">

Academician • Researcher • Editor

I am Dr. Abhijeet Dawle, an Assistant Professor in the Basic Sciences & Humanities Department at SVKM's NMIMS – Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering (MPSTME), Shirpur.

My academic journey is interdisciplinary, bringing together English Language & Literature, Communication, Management, Human Resource Management, Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Education.

My research explores how technology and human-centric approaches can reshape education, workplaces, HR practices, communication, and the future of work.

Connecting disciplines to understand people, technology, learning and workplaces.
